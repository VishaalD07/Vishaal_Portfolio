What I do

I like taking a messy, manual process and turning it into a working AI pipeline. Lately that's been an LLM task parser that turns plain English into structured data, a five-module agent that reads medical documents and acts through Calendar, Gmail and Slack, and a job agent that tailors my resume while I sleep.

My background spans full-stack dev (React Native, FastAPI, Django), applied ML research (medical imaging, quantum-hybrid models, energy forecasting) and plenty of practical data work. I'd rather ship a working proof-of-concept and iterate than over-design something in isolation.
